## Brand & Style

### Personality & Tone
The brand communicates an atmosphere of effortless sanctuary, quiet reassurance, and domestic order. Designed for thoughtful individuals, caregivers, and modern families, it acts as a peaceful refuge from chaotic digital clutter. The tone is deeply human, protective without being paranoid, and completely free of cold cryptographic jargon or clinical cybersecurity aesthetics. Terms like "vault," "keys," or "encryption protocols" are recast into gentle, natural metaphors like "Safe on this device," "Kept close," and "Only yours."

### Visual Aesthetic
The style is a synthesis of Warm Organic Minimalism and Soft Tactile Modernism:
- **Materiality:** Soft eggshell, oatmeal, and stone surfaces that mimic tactile stationery and linen cardstock rather than cold glass or aluminum.
- **Demeanor:** Quiet confidence. Generous negative space, restrained structural lines, and gentle contrast that calms eye strain.
- **Accents:** Muted botanical sage and comforting deep forest tones signal security, continuity, and organic safety.

## Colors

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#2F5D50` - Deep Forest Sage):** Evokes quiet stability, protection, and personal longevity. Used for primary interactions, safe state indicators, and focal navigation nodes.
- **Secondary (`#D17D58` - Warm Terracotta):** Infuses gentle warmth and human vitality. Reserved for highlights, favorite markers, pinned family keepsakes, and organic callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#8EA68B` - Soft Lichen):** A tranquil botanical tone used for gentle badge fills, tag accents, ambient progress rings, and passive state indicators.
- **Neutral Base (`#F9F6F0` - Warm Oat Linen):** The ambient canvas background, preventing harsh screen glare and providing a warm, paper-like foundation.
- **Ink & Typography:** 
  - Primary text utilizes Deep Espresso Charcoal (`#221F1D`), providing absolute legibility while remaining softer and more natural than pitch black.
  - Secondary text utilizes Muted Umber (`#69645E`) for timestamps, subtle metadata, and secondary notes.
  - Tertiary outlines and dividers leverage Stone Wash (`#E7E2D8`), keeping separation gentle and organic.

### Dark Mode Adaptation
In low-light environments, the canvas shifts to Deep Peat (`#1A1918`) with surface containers of Warm Slate Bark (`#242220`), maintaining charcoal depth rather than pure OLED black, preserving the gentle organic quality of the space.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all typographic levels. Its friendly modern geometry, subtle humanist stroke endings, and open counters provide warmth without compromising clarity or structured legibility.

### Rhythmic Guidance
- **Headlines:** Use tighter negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) on display sizes to create an editorial, composed presence. Maintain comfortable line heights to let titles breathe.
- **Body:** Body text uses an uncompressed, open rhythm with generous leading (`line-height: 1.5` to `1.55`) to facilitate fatigue-free reading of longer journal entries, personal reflections, and family milestones.
- **Labels:** Micro-copy, metadata timestamps, and safe indicators are set with gentle positive tracking (`0.01em` to `0.03em`) to enhance legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
The spatial system centers on calm containment. Content floats within airy, well-proportioned margins that avoid crowding the user's perception. 

- **Mobile Viewports (< 640px):** Single-column layout with 4-column sub-grids, bounded by `margin: 1.25rem`. Interactions focus on thumb reachability within bottom sheets and natural card stacks.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with a side navigation rail, prioritizing split views (e.g., memory timeline on the left, full rich entry details on the right).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Max container width capped at 1200px across a 12-column grid to maintain intimate line lengths and eliminate vast, uninviting digital voids.

### Spacing Cadence
Elements follow an 8pt-based rhythm (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Card interiors prioritize `space-md` to `space-lg` padding to convey spaciousness and dignity for personal artifacts.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Stratification
Visual depth is defined by tactile paper layering and ambient tone rather than stark drop shadows:
- **Base Canvas:** Neutral Warm Oat Linen (`#F9F6F0`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Resting Cards):** Warm Alabaster (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle 1px border using Stone Wash (`#E7E2D8`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Selected / Active Modals):** Pure Clean Ivory (`#FFFFFF`) lifted by an ultra-soft, warm umber ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(34, 31, 29, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(34, 31, 29, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Floating Sheets & Controls):** Defined with soft ambient blur and an understated shadow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(34, 31, 29, 0.06)`.

### Tactile Boundary Details
No hard black strokes or high-contrast demarcations are permitted. Outlines remain delicate, soft-edged, and organic.

## Shapes

### Shape Language
A roundedness tier of `2` provides balanced, friendly warmth. Rounded corners remove visual sharpness and invite physical touch, reinforcing an atmosphere of psychological safety.

- **Standard Buttons, Chips, and Form Inputs:** `0.75rem` to `1rem` radius.
- **Memory Cards & Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` corner radius, creating an organic card feel.
- **Modals & Bottom Drawers (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` top corner radius for an inviting sheet aesthetic.
- **Badges & Safe Indicators:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`) for quick tactile scanning.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Deep Forest Sage (`#2F5D50`) background, Warm Alabaster (`#FFFFFF`) text, subtle 8px vertical padding, 1rem border-radius. Active state produces a gentle compress animation (scale down by 1%).
- **Secondary:** Warm Oat Linen background (`#F3EFE6`), Charcoal Ink (`#221F1D`) text, subtle border in Stone Wash (`#E7E2D8`).
- **Gentle Action (Ghost):** No border, tinted Deep Forest Sage text, background transitions to translucent sage (`rgba(47, 93, 80, 0.06)`) on hover/touch.

### Memory & Note Cards
- **Structure:** Surface white containers with generous padding (`space-lg`), soft rounded corners (`rounded-lg`), and Stone Wash border.
- **Header:** Timestamp and human-friendly collection tag ("Family Journal", "Recipe", "Private Thought") followed by primary title.
- **Safe Badge:** A small, delicate pill positioned neatly in the footer or header featuring an unobtrusive botanical green dot alongside the copy "Saved on device."

### Chips & Filters
- Compact pill forms (`rounded-full`) with `label-md` typography.
- **Inactive:** Transparent background, Stone Wash outline, Charcoal Ink text.
- **Active:** Soft Lichen tint (`rgba(142, 166, 139, 0.18)`), Forest Sage border, Forest Sage text.

### Form Inputs
- **Base Style:** Ivory white background, 1.5px border in Stone Wash (`#E7E2D8`), 0.75rem corner radius, generous touch area (minimum 48px height).
- **Focus State:** Border smoothly shifts to Primary Forest Sage (`#2F5D50`) with an ambient halo of `0 0 0 3px rgba(47, 93, 80, 0.12)`.
- **Placeholder:** Muted Umber at 60% opacity, written in a conversational, friendly voice (e.g., "Write a memory, thought, or note...").

### Checkboxes & Toggles
- Rounded squares (6px radius) for checkboxes, smoothly animated pill switches for toggles.
- Checked state uses Forest Sage fill with an organic ivory checkmark. Toggles utilize a warm, buttery thumb container moving over an earthy neutral track.

### Reassurance Callouts
- Specialized soft containers utilizing a delicate wash of Tertiary Lichen (`rgba(142, 166, 139, 0.12)`) and a thin border.
- Employs conversational reassurance: "Kept safe on this device. Backed up to your personal private drive only."
